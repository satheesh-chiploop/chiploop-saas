The required synthesizable top module is adaptive_aero_control_top. Do not substitute a status, telemetry, register-bank, or leaf module as the design top. The top must implement the selected Physical AI application contract, external model request/response transport, validation, safety, timeout, fallback, and bounded actuator-command behavior.
Design an FPGA-synthesizable top module named adaptive_aero_control_top. The RTL shall implement a control wrapper around an external DoMINO automotive-aerodynamics inference service and shall not implement the surrogate model itself.

1. Top-level protocol
- clk and rst_n shall control all sequential logic.
- The design shall provide a single bounded request stream to the external inference service using req_valid/req_ready/req_data[127:0].
- The design shall accept a single bounded response stream from the external inference service using rsp_valid/rsp_ready/rsp_data[127:0].
- The design shall expose a small CSR window using csr_we, csr_re, csr_addr[7:0], csr_wdata[31:0], csr_rdata[31:0].
- The design shall emit a compact actuator command using actuator_cmd_valid and actuator_cmd_data[31:0].
- The design shall emit compact status using status_flags[31:0].

2. Request handling
- The design shall assemble each outbound request word from local control inputs and CSR-configured parameters, including at minimum a monotonically incrementing request sequence number, a current operating mode, a velocity field derived from the configured stream velocity, and a metadata field identifying the reference geometry source.
- The design shall only assert req_valid when a request has passed local validation and there is no outstanding unacknowledged request that would violate the configured transaction policy.
- The design shall hold req_data stable while req_valid is asserted and req_ready is deasserted.
- The design shall increment the request sequence only upon successful request acceptance by req_ready.

3. Response handling
- The design shall capture rsp_data only when rsp_valid and rsp_ready are asserted in the same cycle.
- The design shall parse the response word into at least: response sequence ID, command payload, response validity, health indicator, and freshness metadata.
- The design shall compare the response sequence ID against the outstanding request sequence and reject the response if they do not match.
- The design shall reject any response that is marked invalid or unhealthy.
- The design shall reject any response received after timeout expiration.

4. Timeout and freshness
- The design shall include a programmable timeout counter loaded from CSR.
- The timeout counter shall begin when a request is accepted and shall count until a valid matching response is accepted or a timeout occurs.
- When the timeout expires, the design shall enter safe fallback mode and shall mark the request as stale.
- The design shall latch stale-response and timeout indicators in status_flags until cleared by reset or CSR-defined clear action.

5. Actuator command generation
- The design shall translate validated response command payloads into actuator_cmd_data.
- The design shall clamp actuator_cmd_data to programmable minimum and maximum limits from CSR.
- The design shall assert actuator_cmd_valid only when the command is validated, current, and within freshness constraints.
- If no valid command is available, the design shall output a deterministic safe fallback actuator command and deassert actuator_cmd_valid or assert it with a dedicated fallback-valid indication encoded in status_flags, depending on the CSR-configured policy.

6. Safety and fallback
- The design shall include a safe fallback manager that forces the actuator to a safe position on reset, timeout, stale data, invalid response, or explicit fault condition.
- The safe fallback value shall be programmable via CSR and shall be used whenever the model data path cannot be trusted.
- The design shall reject stale model commands and shall never allow an out-of-date response to override a newer outstanding request.
- The design shall clamp actuator commands before issuing them to downstream logic.

7. Control/status registers
- The CSR window shall include at least: enable, clear_faults, timeout_threshold, actuator_min, actuator_max, fallback_command, last_error, last_request_id, last_response_id, and status summary bits.
- CSR writes shall update only the addressed configuration fields.
- CSR reads shall return the current configuration and selected status information in a packed 32-bit format.
- Small configuration/state registers and counters are allowed; no large payload storage or tensor memory shall be implemented in registers.

8. Storage and implementation constraints
- The implementation target is FPGA; therefore, if any buffering is required, the design shall use FPGA BRAM abstractions and shall not instantiate Sky130 hard macros.
- The design shall not expose tensors, flow fields, or geometry arrays as top-level ports.
- The design shall not infer bulk payload storage using flip-flop arrays.
- Any buffering shall be limited to compact control words, sequence bookkeeping, and status fields suitable for synthesis.

9. Timing and synthesis
- All sequential logic shall be synthesizable and clocked by clk.
- Reset shall place the design into a known safe fallback state.
- Combinational logic shall be free of latches.
- The design shall preserve ordering and freshness semantics across the request/response path.

10. Integration intent
- The RTL top level shall serve as the hardware control wrapper for a remote NVIDIA DoMINO automotive-aerodynamics inference service running in software or on GPU.
- The RTL shall not attempt to compute drag, lift, pressure, or flow-field predictions itself.
FPGA MEMORY CONTRACT (mandatory): describe bulk payload/history/FIFO storage through a technology-neutral memory wrapper that the FPGA flow can map to native block RAM; do not flatten it into registers.
