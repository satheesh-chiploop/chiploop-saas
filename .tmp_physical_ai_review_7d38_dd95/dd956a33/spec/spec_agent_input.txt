Design a synthesizable ASIC digital IP block named aero_guard_ctrl that implements a deterministic active-aerodynamics supervision and command-synthesis pipeline around an external DoMINO model service. The RTL shall not include any physics inference engine, neural network, or GPU functionality. The block shall provide valid/ready style handshaking at its input and output boundaries and shall support a single outstanding model request at a time unless otherwise parameterized.

1. Clocking and reset
- The design shall use a single primary clock clk and active-low synchronous reset_n unless a project wrapper specifies otherwise.
- On reset, all internal registers shall initialize to a safe state: request_valid=0, outstanding_request=0, fallback_active=1, actuator_enable=0, command outputs set to conservative defaults, stale_reject=0, timeout_flag=0, clamp_active=0.

2. Input capture and request formation
- The block shall accept geometry descriptor inputs vehicle_geometry_in and flow_conditions_in.
- The geometry interface shall consume compact descriptor fields only; no parsing of full STL meshes or floating-point geometry computation shall be implemented in RTL.
- The block shall assemble a request packet containing request_id, geometry descriptor fields, stream_velocity_mps, and mode flags for export to model_request_out.
- request_id shall increment monotonically for each accepted request launch and shall be stored for response matching.
- The request launch shall only occur when the controller is not in fallback lockout and no newer request is outstanding.

3. Outstanding request tracking and stale rejection
- A request_age counter shall increment every cycle while a request is outstanding.
- A programmable TIMEOUT_CYCLES register shall define the maximum allowed response age.
- If request_age exceeds TIMEOUT_CYCLES before a matching response is received, the design shall assert timeout_flag, clear request_pending, and force fallback_active.
- A model response shall be accepted only if response.request_id equals the stored outstanding request_id and the response status indicates valid completion.
- Any response with mismatched request_id, missing payload, or late arrival after timeout shall be discarded and shall assert stale_reject.

4. Response validation
- The response capture path shall latch drag_force, lift_force, surface_pressure summary fields, and a compact flow_field status token if present.
- The design shall treat surface_pressure and flow_field as control metadata, not as large memory blobs. The implementation may store only compressed summary fields or flags.
- The response validity filter shall verify header match, completeness, and freshness before releasing values to command synthesis.
- Invalid or absent values shall not propagate to command synthesis and shall instead route the controller to fallback.

5. Command synthesis
- The Actuator Command Synthesis block shall map validated model outputs and current flow conditions to digital actuator setpoints for the active-aerodynamics surfaces.
- The mapping shall be deterministic, combinational or pipelined, and free of floating-point neural inference semantics.
- The command interface shall output at minimum surface_select, position_setpoint, enable, and fault_flag.
- The design shall support a configurable safe command range defined by CMD_MIN and CMD_MAX registers or generics.
- The command synthesis output shall be registered before the clamp stage.

6. Clamp and rate limiting
- A command clamp stage shall enforce CMD_MIN <= position_setpoint <= CMD_MAX.
- If a synthesized command exceeds bounds, the design shall saturate it to the nearest limit and assert clamp_active.
- If enabled by parameter, a rate limiter shall limit setpoint delta per cycle to RATE_MAX_DELTA.
- Rate-limited or clamped commands shall still remain safe and valid for downstream actuation.

7. Safe fallback behavior
- The Safe Fallback Controller shall generate conservative actuator commands independent of the model response path.
- Fallback shall be asserted when no model response is available, when timeout occurs, when stale_reject is asserted, when response validity fails, or when an external fault input is asserted.
- In fallback mode, actuator_enable shall be driven to a safe value and the setpoint shall be forced to a predefined conservative position encoded in SAFE_POSITION or equivalent.
- The design shall provide a manual_override or safe_mode input if required by the integration wrapper, but model-derived commands shall never bypass safety checks.

8. Output interface
- The output interface shall register final actuator commands before driving actuator_cmd_out.
- The design shall expose safe_fallback_out as either a separate port or encoded fault/enable combination, depending on integration style, but the fallback state shall always be externally observable.
- The design shall not drive unknown or high-impedance values on synthesizable outputs.

9. Status and observability
- The block shall provide status flags valid, busy, stale_reject, clamp_active, fallback_active, timeout_flag, and fault_flag for system integration and verification.
- Status flags shall be updated synchronously and shall reflect the final decision of the control pipeline each cycle.

10. Parameterization
- The design shall expose parameters or generics for TIMEOUT_CYCLES, CMD_MIN, CMD_MAX, SAFE_POSITION, RATE_MAX_DELTA, and optionally SINGLE_OUTSTANDING_REQUEST.
- The design shall be synthesizable in ASIC flows and shall avoid unbounded loops, dynamic allocation, file I/O, and unsynthesizable real-number operations.

11. Environmental assumptions
- The controller shall accept flow_conditions for stream velocity within the configured operating envelope of 20 to 55 m/s.
- The reference condition stream_velocity_mps=38.89 and geometry_source=DrivAerML reference geometry shall be treated as configuration context for verification and integration, not as hard-coded inference outputs.

12. External service boundary
- The RTL shall terminate at a service boundary for the NVIDIA DoMINO model runtime. Any GPU worker, remote NIM, or network transport shall be implemented outside this IP.
- The model interface shall be treated as an asynchronous external functional block with request/response semantics.

13. Safety intent
- The design shall reject stale model commands, clamp actuator commands, and provide a safe fallback at all times.
- If the controller cannot guarantee validity of the current command, it shall output the safe fallback rather than attempting to reuse an expired result.
- All safety behavior shall be deterministic, synthesizable, and suitable for formal verification and gate-level implementation.
