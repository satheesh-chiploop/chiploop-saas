Create a synthesizable digital IP block named domino_active_aero_controller suitable for ASIC implementation. The RTL shall not contain the physics model itself, neural network layers, or any non-deterministic software runtime. The block shall implement a request/response orchestration pipeline for an external NVIDIA DoMINO Automotive Aero runtime.

1. Top-level ports and clocks
- Provide input clk and active-low rst_n.
- Provide input vehicle_geometry_valid and vehicle_geometry_ready handshake signals, and a bounded-width vehicle_geometry bus carrying preprocessed geometry descriptors.
- Provide input flow_conditions_valid and flow_conditions_ready handshake signals, and a flow_conditions bus containing at least stream_velocity_mps.
- Provide output model_req_valid and input model_req_ready for requests to the external runtime.
- Provide output model_resp_ready and input model_resp_valid for responses from the external runtime.
- Provide response payload inputs for drag_force, lift_force, surface_pressure, and flow_field metadata or compacted descriptors as architecturally agreed.
- Provide output actuator_cmd_valid, input actuator_cmd_ready, and actuator_cmd bus to the active-aerodynamics actuator subsystem.
- Provide output fault_valid and fault_code for diagnostic reporting.

2. Operating envelope and configuration
- The design shall accept stream_velocity_mps values corresponding to the specified operational envelope of 20 to 55 m/s.
- A configuration register shall store the reference-condition metadata, including a default stream_velocity_mps reference of 38.89 m/s and the geometry format provenance tag.
- The geometry input is architecture-defined as STL-derived DrivAerML reference geometry; RTL may accept a compact descriptor or hash/index of the geometry rather than raw STL parsing.
- The design shall include a parameterizable fixed-point format for all internal arithmetic; all widths shall be synthesis-time generics.

3. Request sequencing and freshness
- The request controller shall issue a monotonically increasing request_id for every model transaction.
- Every request shall carry request_id and a timestamp or cycle_age tag.
- The response ingress shall compare the response request_id with the outstanding request_id and shall assert response_match only when the IDs align.
- The freshness monitor shall reject responses that exceed a programmable freshness threshold or violate sequence ordering.
- If a response is stale, duplicated, missing, or mismatched, the design shall suppress model-derived actuator output and assert fault_valid with a stale-command fault code.

4. Model interaction behavior
- The RTL shall only marshal inputs to and from the external runtime; it shall not perform local surrogate inference.
- The model request payload shall include vehicle_geometry and flow_conditions fields only in the architecturally defined format.
- The response payload shall accept drag_force, lift_force, surface_pressure, and flow_field summary data.
- The design shall provide a response_valid internal qualifier that is set only after payload integrity and ID checks pass.

5. Command synthesis
- A command synthesis engine shall map validated model outputs into actuator requests using deterministic fixed-point arithmetic.
- The command mapping shall be parameterizable by application-specific gains or tables stored in ROM or configuration registers.
- The exact control law may be simple feedforward or table-based, but it must be synthesizable and fully deterministic.
- No actuator command shall be driven directly from unvalidated model output signals.

6. Actuator clamping
- The actuator command clamper shall enforce min and max limits on every actuator channel.
- If a computed command exceeds limits, the clamper shall saturate the value to the nearest legal bound and set a clamp_event flag.
- Clamp events shall be visible to fault reporting and telemetry.
- The unclamped path must not be exposed at the output interface.

7. Safe fallback
- The safe_fallback_manager shall implement a deterministic state machine that outputs a predefined safe aerodynamic configuration when any of the following occur: reset, invalid input, stale response, request timeout, model unavailable, payload corruption, or internal fault.
- The safe fallback configuration shall be encoded as synthesis-time constants or configuration registers.
- On reset, the design shall enter fallback mode and hold safe outputs until health checks and valid data are present.
- Safe fallback outputs shall also pass through the actuator clamper.

8. Safety supervision and arbitration
- The safety_supervisor shall arbitrate between model-derived commands and fallback commands.
- The supervisor shall prioritize fallback on any fault, mismatch, stale condition, or timeout.
- A direct model-to-actuator bypass path is prohibited.
- The supervisor shall assert a source_select status indicating whether the output originated from model or fallback.

9. Timing and latency
- All state transitions shall be synchronous to clk.
- All state elements shall have synchronous behavior compatible with ASIC synthesis.
- The design shall support a bounded-latency handshake from request issuance to response acceptance, but no numeric performance guarantee shall be assumed beyond the configured watchdog thresholds.

10. Observability and diagnostics
- The design shall expose telemetry signals for request_id, response_match, freshness status, clamp_event, fallback_active, and fault_code.
- Telemetry registers may be memory-mapped if integrated into a larger SoC, but the controller shall remain self-contained.
- Fault reporting shall be sticky until cleared by software or a defined clear mechanism.

11. Reset and initialization
- On rst_n deassertion, all outputs shall be driven to safe values.
- The request controller shall clear outstanding transactions on reset.
- The design shall require a successful configuration load, if applicable, before leaving fallback mode.

12. Synthesis constraints
- The RTL shall be written using synthesizable constructs only: flip-flops, combinational logic, synchronous FSMs, counters, comparators, saturating arithmetic, and optional small ROMs.
- No floating-point requirement shall be assumed unless explicitly implemented as a synthesizable fixed-point approximation.
- No file I/O, dynamic memory, tasks with side effects, or unsynthesizable delays shall be used.
- All default values, safe fallback settings, limits, and thresholds shall be parameterizable.

13. Functional intent
- The IP shall serve as an ASIC control wrapper around an external GPU-based or hosted NIM DoMINO runtime for active aerodynamics.
- The IP shall preserve safety by rejecting stale commands, clamping actuator commands, and providing safe fallback under all fault or invalid-data conditions.
