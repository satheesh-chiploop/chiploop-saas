Create a synthesizable digital IP block named active_aero_controller_wrap for ASIC integration. The block shall implement a synchronous, resettable control plane that accepts vehicle_geometry and flow_conditions inputs, generates model_request transactions for an external DoMINO model service, validates model_response transactions, computes actuator targets from validated outputs, clamps outputs to safe bounds, and provides a safe fallback state when required.

1. Clocking and reset
- The design shall use a single primary clock and an active-high synchronous reset, or equivalent ASIC-friendly synchronous reset scheme.
- All state elements shall be clocked. No combinational feedback loops shall exist.
- On reset, the block shall clear outstanding request state, invalid/stale flags, fault counters, and shall drive actuator outputs to the defined safe fallback state.

2. Input handling
- The vehicle_geometry input shall be accepted as a framed payload or memory-mapped descriptor corresponding to the supplied STL-based geometry source. The RTL shall not implement STL parsing algorithms beyond simple framing, length checking, and storage of payload descriptors needed for service requests.
- The flow_conditions input shall include a numeric stream velocity field expressed in m/s and optional control metadata.
- The flow_conditions field shall be range-checked against the operating envelope 20 to 55 m/s. Values outside this range shall assert an invalid-input fault and shall inhibit actuation updates until corrected.
- Inputs shall be sampled into registers before request generation.

3. Request generation
- The block shall generate model_request packets containing: request_valid, request_id/sequence number, geometry descriptor or payload handle, flow-condition payload, and any required timestamp or age metadata.
- Each request shall increment or otherwise advance a monotonic sequence counter.
- Only one outstanding request shall be required by the baseline architecture unless the implementation chooses to support deeper pipelining; if pipelining is supported, each response shall be matched by ID.
- The request interface shall be compatible with a future NVIDIA-hosted NIM or ChipLoop GPU worker runtime and shall remain independent of the runtime implementation.

4. Response validation
- The block shall accept model_response transactions containing drag_force, lift_force, surface_pressure, and flow_field data fields.
- The validator shall compare response sequence ID against the most recent outstanding request sequence.
- The validator shall reject stale responses, duplicated responses, out-of-order responses, malformed responses, or responses received after a configurable timeout.
- A stale response, timeout, or malformed response shall trigger fault_status and safe_fallback_request.
- The design shall not make any assumptions about the physics values beyond their presence and format; it shall not encode surrogate-model predictions in RTL.

5. Control law and command generation
- The control law block shall consume validated model outputs and generate desired actuator targets for the active-aerodynamics system.
- The control law shall be deterministic, fully synthesizable, and based on configurable arithmetic, lookup, or threshold logic.
- The architecture shall support one or more actuator channels, each with its own target value path, but the exact plant behavior is outside the RTL scope.
- If validated model data is unavailable, the control law shall not update actuator targets and shall defer to safe fallback.

6. Clamp and rate limiting
- All actuator target values shall pass through a clamp block before being sent to outputs.
- The clamp block shall enforce programmable minimum and maximum limits for each actuator channel.
- If commanded value exceeds bounds, the block shall saturate to the nearest bound and set a clamp event flag.
- Optional rate limiting may be included as a synthesizable feature; if implemented, it shall be monotonic and bounded by programmable delta limits.

7. Safe fallback behavior
- The safe fallback supervisor shall monitor invalid-input, stale-response, timeout, and interface-fault conditions.
- On any unsafe condition, it shall assert safe_fallback_request and cause actuator outputs to transition to a predefined safe state.
- The safe state shall be a registered value, such as hold-last-safe or park-position, as configured by system integration.
- Fallback shall persist until the block receives a valid, fresh, sequence-matched model_response and the validator clears the fault condition.

8. Outputs and diagnostics
- actuator_command outputs shall be registered and glitch-free at the interface boundary.
- fault_status shall expose at least: input range fault, request outstanding, response valid, stale reject, timeout, clamp active, and fallback active.
- A diagnostics register file shall be provided for system software to read live and sticky fault indicators.

9. Timing and synthesis constraints
- All major paths shall be partitioned into registers so the design is suitable for ASIC synthesis.
- The design shall not require GPU logic, floating-point accelerator internals, or any neural-network inference hardware.
- Any arithmetic used in the control law shall be explicitly bounded and synthesizable in standard-cell ASIC flows.
- The block shall tolerate the specified operating envelope without changing RTL structure; only configuration values such as thresholds and clamp limits may vary.

10. Integration contract
- The block shall expose a clear transaction boundary for the external DoMINO model service.
- The external model service is not part of the RTL deliverable; the RTL only manages handshake, freshness, and safe use of returned model data.
- The implementation shall support future connection to either NVIDIA-hosted NIM or ChipLoop GPU worker without modification to the core safety logic.
